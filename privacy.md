# Privacy Policy: Venture Vinnie

*Last updated: 28 September 2026*

Venture Vinnie is a personal tool used only by its owner, Jac Robertson, to research product ideas for an Etsy shop. It is not offered to the public.

## What it accesses

It reads Pinterest's public trend data (popular and growing search keywords in the UK) through the Pinterest API. It does not access other users' accounts, boards, pins or personal information. It does not post, pin or change anything on Pinterest.

## What it stores

Only trend keywords and their growth figures, kept on the owner's own computer. No personal data about Pinterest users is collected, stored, sold or shared.

## Third parties

Data is not shared with anyone.

## Deleting data

Cached trend data can be deleted at any time. The app's access can be revoked at any time from Pinterest's settings.
